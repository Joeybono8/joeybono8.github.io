Right-Sized TIG Welder

UConn Senior Design (ME38), sponsored by Pursuit Aerospace. My team is building a compact, automated TIG welding station to replace oversized welding machines on an aerospace sheet metal line. It has to be rigid, repeatable, production ready, and small enough to fit in under 36.4 sq ft of floor space.

  • Concept and layout Starting point Table-style layout chosen with the 3P design process, tested with cardboard and Creform mockups, and modeled in CAD by the previous team.
  • Assembly and controls In progress Putting the sourced power source, cooler, wire feeder and controls together, and refining the pneumatic clamping system.
  • Frame and top assembly In progress Designing and building the upper portion of the machine, along with CAD updates and fabrication.
  • Weld testing Upcoming Demonstration welds to confirm repeatability, then rebuilding where needed to meet requirements by demo day in April 2027.

Details are limited because the project is covered by a sponsor NDA and IP agreement. I am happy to talk through my role in person.
